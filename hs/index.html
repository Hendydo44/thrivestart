Industry-specific levers

Different businesses, different cash drivers. Here's what I focus on.

  • Annual price increases - Most operators leave 5-10% on the table every year
  • Autopay and prepaid annual plans - Predictable cash and lower churn
  • Cancellation rate - Every point of churn you cut is pure margin
  • Revenue per tech - Are your routes dense enough?
  • Chemical cost as % of revenue - Small changes add up fast
  • Winter cash planning - Build the reserve before November
  • Maintenance vs. enhancement mix - Recurring revenue is worth more
  • Crew labor % - Track it weekly, not monthly
  • Job costing on installs - Know your actual margin before you bid
  • Equipment financing - Match payments to useful life
