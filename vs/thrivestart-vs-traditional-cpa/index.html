When to Choose ThriveStart

You're Running a Deal Process

When M&A and CFO work together, due diligence is faster, data rooms are cleaner, and deal structuring reflects operational reality. No translation layer between firms.

You Value Simplicity

One team, one invoice, one Slack channel. No scheduling calls between your CPA, M&A advisor, and capital advisor to get everyone aligned.

You're Cost-Conscious

Traditional M&A advisors charge $10,000–$15,000/month in retainers alone. ThriveStart's M&A service is $5,000/month plus success fee—less than half the retainer cost.

You Need Speed

Onboarding a CPA firm takes weeks. Adding an M&A advisor takes more weeks. ThriveStart can be working on your deal within 48 hours.

When to Choose Traditional Firms

You're $50M+ Revenue

Large, complex organizations may need dedicated teams at each firm. At $50M+ revenue, the cost of multiple vendors is more easily absorbed.

You Need Full-Service Audit

ThriveStart doesn't do audits. If you need audited financials for compliance or institutional investors, you'll need a CPA firm regardless.

You Want Big-Brand Names

Some sponsors or investors want to see Big 4 QoE or a name-brand investment bank. ThriveStart is boutique by design.

You Have Existing Relationships

If your CPA has been with you for 15 years and you trust them deeply, adding ThriveStart for M&A-specific work makes sense—we can complement, not replace.
