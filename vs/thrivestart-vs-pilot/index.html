When to Choose ThriveStart

You're Acquiring or Selling a Business

ThriveStart offers full M&A advisory: target identification, LOI drafting, due diligence, deal structuring, and integration support. Pilot doesn't offer M&A services.

You're Raising Capital

We build lender decks, run outreach pipelines, and negotiate term sheets. Pilot offers fundraising support in their CFO tier, but not dedicated capital advisory.

You're PE-Backed or Sponsor-Owned

ThriveStart speaks the language of PE: QoE, 13-week cash flow, covenant compliance, and board reporting. We're built for sponsor requirements.

You're in a Cash Crisis

Financial workouts, creditor negotiations, and SBA distressed situations are core ThriveStart services. Pilot focuses on growth-stage bookkeeping.

When to Choose Pilot

You Need Bookkeeping First

If your books are a mess and you need basic accounting cleanup before strategic finance, Pilot's bookkeeping tier ($99/month) is a good starting point.

You Need Tax Filing

Pilot offers integrated tax filing for LLCs, S-Corps, and C-Corps. ThriveStart focuses on strategic finance—you'd work with a CPA for tax.

You're Pre-Revenue or Early Stage

Pilot's pre-revenue discounts and bookkeeping focus suit companies still finding product-market fit. ThriveStart targets $2M+ revenue companies.

Budget is the Primary Constraint

Pilot's CFO tier starts at $1,750/month. ThriveStart starts at $3,500/month—but includes M&A-ready capabilities Pilot doesn't offer.
