Straight answers

I've never bought a company before. Is that a problem?

No. That's who we're built for. First-time independent sponsors don't need a banker; they need someone who's been through LOI-to-close and the first year after it, and who can make lenders and investors take them seriously.

Will you replace my bookkeeper or CPA?

No. We automate the manual work around your bookkeeper so they can focus on accuracy and exceptions, and we coordinate with your CPA on tax. We add the CFO layer that's missing: forecasting, margin analysis, and decision support.

Is the AI real or a buzzword?

It's real, and it's specific: transaction categorization, AP capture and approval, AR follow-up, GL-level diligence analysis, and plain-English answers from your own numbers. A senior human reviews everything that matters.

How is this different from hiring a fractional CFO?

Most fractional CFOs sell open-ended hours. We scope every engagement to an outcome: a closed deal, or a finance function that runs itself. And we leave behind systems, not just reports.

How fast can you start?

Most engagements kick off within 48 to 72 hours. If you have a deal deadline, we can be in your data room within 24 hours.

What does it cost?

Every engagement is scoped to the deal or the outcome, not open-ended hours. After the first call you get a fixed proposal in writing, backed by our 30-day money-back guarantee.

A 15-minute call with a senior partner, not a sales rep. You'll leave with a clear next step whether or not we work together.
