Questions owners ask us

Do I have to switch accounting software?

Almost never. We work with QuickBooks, Xero, and the field-service and ops software you already use. Automation tools are layered on top, not swapped in.

Will my team have to learn a bunch of new things?

Very little. Approvals happen from a phone, and reports show up in your inbox. We handle the setup, training, and anything that breaks.

Is my financial data safe with AI tools?

We use established, bank-grade accounting automation platforms with proper access controls. Your data isn't used to train public AI models, and you own all of it.

We're profitable. Why do we need this?

Profitable companies leave the most money on the table: underpriced customers, slow collections, and cash tied up where you can't see it. You'll also get a higher price if you ever sell.

What does it cost compared with hiring?

A fraction of a full-time controller, and far less than a $250K–$300K CFO. You'll get a fixed monthly proposal after the assessment, backed by our 30-day money-back guarantee.
